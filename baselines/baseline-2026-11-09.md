## Methodology + caveats

- **Channel resolution:** All 13 active channels scanned via uploads playlist (playlistId derived by replacing 'UC' prefix with 'UU' in channelId).
- **Recent uploads:** Derived from the uploads playlist (playlistItems.list, maxResults=50), then filtered and enriched with videos.list for duration and view counts.
- **Shorts classification:** `duration < 60s`. Shorts median is `n/a` when no shorts appear in the 50-video sample.
- **Outlier threshold:** `long-form median × 3` per spec. Use this in next month's outlier scan to flag breakout videos.
- **Subscriber counts:** Fetched via channels.list (one batched call for all 13 channels).

## Read of the data

- **Cadence leaders this cycle:** **Michael Bordenaro (3.89/wk)**, **Reventure Consulting (3.66/wk)**, **Flipping Mastery TV (3.42/wk)** remain the most active publishers. The cohort overall is maintaining steady output, with most channels at 1–3 long-form uploads per week.

- **Strongest median views (consistently winning, not one-hit):** **Graham Stephan (503,059)** leads all real-estate-native channels in median long-form views. **Michael Bordenaro (181,461)** holds second. These channels are setting the benchmark for what resonates at scale.

- **Notable threshold shifts:** Freedom Mentor's threshold moved up significantly (630 → 1,350, +114%), indicating a meaningful change in median performance. Watch for reversion or continuation next cycle.

- **Shorts vs. long-form dynamics:** Most channels still generate higher median views on long-form vs. Shorts. Channels where Shorts median exceeds long-form median are worth monitoring for format-shift signals.

- **Active cohort:** No channels are currently dormant (all posted within 30 days). The competitor set is fully engaged.
