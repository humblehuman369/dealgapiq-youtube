Reference date for cadence/recency cutoffs: **2026-05-09** (90d window starts 2026-02-08, 30d window starts 2026-04-09).

## Methodology + caveats

- **Channel resolution:** All 15 channels resolved to a verified channelId via YouTube search. Roofstock did not surface its own channel from the @handle pattern; located it via a secondary "Roofstock + Memphis" search (channelId `UC3C867XTb4PISoUzmtxkMcg`).
- **Recent uploads:** The MCP server exposes channel statistics, top-by-views, and keyword search — but **not** a direct "list channel uploads chronologically" endpoint. Recent-upload metrics (90d count, last-30 medians, cadence) were derived by searching for each channel's name with high `maxResults`, filtering hits to the channel's own `channelId`, sorting by `publishedAt` desc, then pulling per-video `contentDetails.duration` and `statistics.viewCount`. This is YouTube **search-relevance ordering**, not the uploads playlist, so channels whose names are unique and whose videos use their name in titles/descriptions (most of this list) yield close-to-complete coverage; for sparser matches the metrics are best-effort.
- **Shorts classification:** `duration < 60s`. Shorts median is `n/a` when no shorts were captured in the recent sample.
- **Outlier threshold:** `long-form median × 3` per spec. Use this in next month's outlier scan to flag breakout videos.

## Read of the data

- **Most consistent publishers (last 90 days):** **BiggerPockets, Michael Bordenaro, and Reventure Consulting** all hit ≈1.32 long-form uploads/week — a tight ~5–6 day cadence that's the clear high-end of the cohort. Flipping Mastery (0.62/wk) and Graham Stephan (0.47/wk) come next. Everyone else is below 0.32/wk and most of the SaaS-tool channels are at 0.

- **Strongest median views (consistently winning, not one-hit):** **Graham Stephan (390,800)** is in a different league, but his finance breadth muddies the comp. Among real-estate-native channels, **Reventure Consulting (141,224)** and **Michael Bordenaro (115,264)** are the standouts — both are data-driven housing-market commentary with consistent six-figure medians. Robuilt (44,444) is a strong niche performer.

- **Channels where Shorts median > Long-form median (pivot signal):** **Graham Stephan** is the standout: shorts median (1.87M) is ~4.8× his long-form median (391k) — a real signal that the algorithm is rewarding his shorts more than his main feed. **Freedom Mentor** also has shorts > long-form (578 vs. 458), but on tiny absolute volumes. **BatchTV** narrowly (754 vs. 640). For Graham specifically, this is an actionable competitor signal — worth examining what hooks/topics his shorts use.

- **Closest peers to your likely starting point (study most):**
  1. **Reventure Consulting (Nick Gerli)** — closest spiritual peer to DealGapIQ. Data-driven housing commentary that uses proprietary signals (he runs reventure.app); proves a data tool can fuel an audience-of-six-figures-median content engine. Cadence + median is the model to replicate.
  2. **Michael Bordenaro** — same data-meets-housing-narrative format with a strong personality layer; he's running 1.3 uploads/week and clearing 115k median. Closer to a one-creator setup than Reventure, which makes the playbook more replicable for a small team.
  Brandon Turner (44.7k subs) is the only direct sub-tier match, but he's currently dormant and his content hasn't found product-market-fit since the BiggerPockets exit — useful as a cautionary case, not a model.

- **Dormant channels (no upload in 30+ days — drop from future scans):**
  - **Truly dead:** BatchTV (2025-04-04, 13 mo), DealMachine (2025-09-20, 8 mo). (Roofstock is dropped on positioning grounds, not just dormancy — see Excluded section.)
  - **Recently quiet but may return:** Ryan Pineda (Feb 6), Freedom Mentor (Feb 18), Brandon Turner (Mar 3), Mashvisor (Mar 10), PropStream (Mar 26), Robuilt (Mar 31).
  - **Recommendation:** Drop the two "truly dead" channels permanently. Demote the other six to monthly (not weekly) checks until they post again.
