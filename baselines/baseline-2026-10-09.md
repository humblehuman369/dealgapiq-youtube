## Methodology + caveats

- **Channel resolution:** All 13 active channels scanned via uploads playlist (playlistId derived by replacing 'UC' prefix with 'UU' in channelId). One channel added vs. prior baseline: Flip With Rick (UCrJwaBihSNoEV7y2NWth4Jw), seed threshold 5,000.
- **Recent uploads:** Derived from the uploads playlist (playlistItems.list, maxResults=50), then filtered and enriched with videos.list for duration and view counts. This is more accurate than the search-relevance method used in the May baseline.
- **Shorts classification:** `duration < 60s`. Shorts median is `n/a` when no shorts appear in the 50-video sample.
- **Outlier threshold:** `long-form median × 3` per spec. Use this in next month's outlier scan to flag breakout videos.
- **Subscriber counts:** Fetched via channels.list (one batched call for all 13 channels).

## Read of the data

- **Cadence surge across the board:** Every active channel is posting more frequently than the May baseline showed. The most active publishers in the 90-day window: **Michael Bordenaro (3.89/wk)**, **Reventure Consulting (3.73/wk)**, and **Flipping Mastery TV (3.34/wk)** are running near-daily schedules. BiggerPockets (2.26/wk), PropStream (2.02/wk), Freedom Mentor (2.33/wk), and Flip With Rick (2.33/wk) are all at 2+ per week. The cohort's average cadence has roughly doubled since May — some real, some methodological improvement in measurement.

- **Strongest median views (consistently winning, not one-hit):** **Michael Bordenaro (235,460)** has overtaken Reventure Consulting as the median-view leader among real-estate-native channels, with a striking jump from 115,264 in May. His doom-economy format ("X is PANICKING," "People Stopped Buying Y") is generating consistent six-figure views. **Graham Stephan (396,776)** remains in a different league. **Reventure Consulting (49,060)** has dropped materially from 141,224 — their threshold drops accordingly. **Robuilt (18,425)** moved from dormancy to consistent output with a solid mid-tier median.

- **Notable threshold shifts from May to October:** Several channels changed significantly. Michael Bordenaro's threshold doubled (345,792 → 706,380), reflecting his median surge. Reventure Consulting's dropped sharply (423,672 → 147,180), suggesting more recent videos are pulling lower view counts even as breakout videos still land 500k–1.2M. PropStream's threshold collapsed from 20,295 to 384 — their actual viewership has cratered; the three outliers this scan flagged are largely artifacts of the low bar. Freedom Mentor (1,374 → 630) similarly collapsed in actual viewership despite resumed posting.

- **Channels where Shorts median > Long-form median:** **PropStream** (188 vs 128 — both tiny) and **Mashvisor** (32 vs 31 — negligible). No actionable signal this cycle. Shorts median for Graham Stephan dropped sharply (1,868,636 → 33,965) — possibly a change in Shorts strategy or a data artifact from the smaller sample size in the playlist pull.

- **Resumptions to watch:** **Ryan Pineda** (was dormant since Feb 2026, now 1.48/wk at 425k subs) and **Robuilt** (dormant since Mar 2026, now 1.01/wk at 456k subs) are the highest-impact resumptions. Both have large audiences and relatively low thresholds (20,547 and 55,275 respectively) — watch for breakouts as their new content seasons. **Brandon Turner** resumed with two outlier videos this scan — a rare signal for a sub-50k channel.
