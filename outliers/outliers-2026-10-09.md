## Worth dissecting (top 3 by multiplier)

**1. "How To Reach Financial Freedom In 3 Years Even If You're Broke" — Brandon Turner (4.92x)**
Hook: *"If I lost EVERYTHING tomorrow... my money, properties, businesses..."*
The confessional reset framing — "what would I do if I started over?" — is an evergreen engagement trigger that large audiences at any level can project onto. Brandon Turner has been mostly quiet since his BiggerPockets exit; the outlier performance here suggests his audience was starved for direct, personal advice rather than podcast-format content. Pattern to note: irreversible loss framing + redemption roadmap consistently outperforms system/strategy titles at sub-50k channels.

**2. "I Don't Want You to Get Rich Anymore" — Brandon Turner (4.67x)**

The tension between persona ("he made millions in real estate") and headline ("he doesn't want you rich") is unresolvable without watching. Two outliers in the same window from the same creator strongly signal audience pent-up demand — worth watching whether Turner sustains this cadence or it's a one-window burst.

Product-launch announcement video hitting 3.3x threshold is anomalous for a SaaS tool channel.
